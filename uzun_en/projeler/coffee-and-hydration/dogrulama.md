## Summary
All six sources were found and match the cited details. Numbers, populations and framing are accurate and appropriately qualified (men only, habitual drinkers, short, one-time dose). One edit: scene 35 now discloses the coffee-industry funding of the Killer trial. Killer's abstract says 3-day trials while its methods describe four days; the script keeps the abstract's wording. Promises and forward references are paid off.
