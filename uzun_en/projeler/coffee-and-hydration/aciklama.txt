Does Coffee Dehydrate You? What Studies Show

Does coffee dehydrate you? In a controlled crossover trial of 50 men who drank coffee every day, four servings of coffee a day hydrated them about as well as water: total body water was 51.5 kilograms on coffee and 51.4 on water. In a second randomized trial, coffee's four-hour urine output did not differ from water's.

In this video we look at where the diuretic myth comes from, the controlled trials that tested it, why regular drinkers adapt, the limits of the evidence, and how much caffeine FDA and EFSA consider safe for most healthy adults.
