## Summary
All 12 cited sources are real and match their citations. Numbers were correct throughout. 11 edits were made, mostly softening wording that went slightly beyond the evidence: the spinach study compared leaf forms, not raw and cooked; the human evidence is for tomato paste only; "barely moved" overstated a 15% lutein loss. Premise is sound, and the script has the needed warfarin note and the lab-versus-people caveats. The two NIH pages could not be opened and were not independently confirmed.
