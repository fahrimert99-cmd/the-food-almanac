Do Bananas Lower Blood Pressure?

Bananas have a reputation as the blood pressure fruit. In a 2013 review of 22 trials, more potassium lowered the top blood pressure number by about 5 points in adults who already had high blood pressure, but not clearly in adults with normal pressure. A large trial of a potassium salt substitute also lowered strokes. But none of this tested bananas themselves, and potassium can be risky for people with kidney disease or certain medicines.

In this video we look at how much potassium a banana really gives you, how potassium and sodium work together, what the meta-analyses and the DASH diet found, where the evidence runs out, who should be careful and why, and five practical ways to get more potassium from food safely.
