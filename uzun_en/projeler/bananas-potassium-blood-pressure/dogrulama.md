## Summary
Every number in the script matches its primary source. I made six wording fixes and one chart sign fix:
- the sign on the normal-BP systolic bar;
- the sodium-to-potassium comparison;
- the attribution of the NSAID claim;
- the editorial claim;
- the hyperkalemia significance statement;
- the thumbnail qualifier.

The framing is appropriately hedged: no banana trial, supplement and salt-substitute evidence, the sodium confound, and safety warnings for kidney disease and medicines. The promises in scenes 2–3, 8 and 23 are paid off later. Limits of this check:
- I did not open the USDA, National Academies, NKF, AHA and Harvard pages myself. They rest on the writer's notes.
- The hyperkalemia exclusions in Neal 2021 are unverified (secondary).
