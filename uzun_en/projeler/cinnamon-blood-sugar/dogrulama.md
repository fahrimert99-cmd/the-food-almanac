## Summary
All numbers, populations and directions of effect match the cited abstracts and agency pages. Framing is appropriately hedged (association, wide intervals, heterogeneity, Khan expression of concern, Crawford without placebo). Five minor edits were made. The hook (−25 mg/dL, A1C not significant, "which cinnamon to buy and how much is too much") is paid off in scenes 14–15 and 33–62. Remaining caveats: the AGES values come from European samples, and Moridpour's SMDs were deliberately not converted to mg/dL.
