Cinnamon and Blood Sugar: What Studies Show

Pooled trials in adults with type 2 diabetes suggest cinnamon may lower fasting blood sugar by roughly 25 milligrams per deciliter, but the effect on A1C is small, uncertain and not consistent across reviews. The cinnamon sold in most American grocery stores is usually cassia, which contains far more coumarin than Ceylon cinnamon, a compound that can affect the liver in sensitive people.

In this video we look at the meta-analyses and trials, why they disagree, what the National Institutes of Health says, how much coumarin is in each type, how much cinnamon in your coffee is reasonable, and why cinnamon is never a substitute for diabetes medication.
