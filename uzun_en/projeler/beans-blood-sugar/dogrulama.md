## Summary
One real error: the "3% at week 8" gas claim misread the Winham abstract, where 3–11% refers to gas on control diets. The claim is replaced with the abstract's actual findings, and the recap is softened to match. The Jenkins 2012 numbers, the framing of the comparison arm, the Sievenpiper and Hafiz limitations, and the 1982 small-study caveat are accurate and honestly framed. Not independently re-verified: Hafiz 2022 details (taken from the writer's full-text notes), and the Sievenpiper per-outcome trial counts (abstract paraphrase only).
