Beans and Lentils: What They Do to Blood Sugar

In a three-month trial of 121 adults with type 2 diabetes, a low glycemic diet that included about a cup of beans and lentils a day lowered A1C by half a point and systolic blood pressure by more than a high wheat fiber diet. But the comparison group improved too, so how much credit do the beans deserve?

In this video we look at the Toronto trial by Jenkins and colleagues, pooled analyses of pulses and blood sugar, the 'second meal effect' that was first seen with lentils, the honest limits of the evidence, and five easy ways to add a cup a day without the gas. For education only, not medical advice.
