Does a 3-Day Juice Cleanse Remove Toxins?

Detox juice cleanses promise to flush out toxins. A 2015 critical review of the evidence found very little clinical evidence for detox diets and knew of no randomized trials of commercial ones. Weight lost on a juice-only plan seems to come mostly from eating far fewer calories, and it tends to return. For older adults, juice-only plans can also carry real risks, and a few kidney injury cases have been reported.

In this video we look at what marketers mean by toxins, how your liver and kidneys already clear waste, what the reviews and a small 3-day juice study found, the risks tied to blood sugar, potassium and oxalate, and what the NIH says does help your liver.
