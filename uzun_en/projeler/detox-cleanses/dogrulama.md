## Summary
Every cited source was found and matches the script on numbers, populations and study types. Framing is appropriately cautious: the case reports are called single cases, the Harvard result is observational, and the Henning study's limits are stated. Safety notes (ask a doctor, never change prescriptions) are present. The hook promises (no randomized trials, risks after 60, what helps the liver) are all paid off. The one fix was the thumbnail wording.
