Is Going Gluten-Free Healthier Without Celiac Disease?

Gluten-free eating has spread far beyond people with celiac disease. In two large Harvard-linked cohorts, adults without celiac disease who ate less gluten did not have fewer heart attacks, and they had a higher risk of type 2 diabetes. Those are associations, not proof of cause, and gluten-free packaged foods often contain less protein and cost far more.

In this video we cover who truly needs a gluten-free diet (celiac disease, wheat allergy and non-celiac gluten sensitivity), what the 26-year heart study and the diabetes study found, what controlled trials say about gluten sensitivity, what gluten-free products actually contain, and why you should get tested before you cut gluten.
