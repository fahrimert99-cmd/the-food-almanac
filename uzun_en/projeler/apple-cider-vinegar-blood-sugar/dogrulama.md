## Summary
All nine cited studies exist, and the details I checked match what the script says. The numbers are correct. The edits are:
- unverified or overreaching phrases softened (scenes 18, 28, 30, 32, 36, 42, 48, 50);
- the insulin/potassium wording aligned with RxList (scene 43).

The hook, the forward references (gastroparesis, scenes 29 → 45) and the promises (scene 3) are all paid off. The framing is appropriately cautious and has the right safety notes. Approved.
