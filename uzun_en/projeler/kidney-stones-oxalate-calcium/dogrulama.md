## Summary
The numbers, populations and study types match their sources. Framing is appropriately hedged:
- The 2002 trial is described as confounded and men-only.
- The cohorts are described as observational.
- Oxalate is described as not a major risk factor.
- Lemon is described as a "maybe".
- The PUSH null result is included.

Three edits were made. The first two concern wording that no accessible guideline summary supports. The third is a clinical-accuracy softening.
1. Scene 43: removed the AUA attribution for animal-protein evidence.
2. Scene 39: the "not all fluid becomes urine" remark is now general reasoning, not a guideline claim.
3. Scene 47: struvite and cystine now read "higher fluid intake plus other treatment".

Safety notes are present (heart or kidney failure and fluid limits, thiazides, supplements, advanced kidney disease, asking a doctor). Verdict: approved.
