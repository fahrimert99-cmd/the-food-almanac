Yogurt and Kefir: What They Do to Your Gut

In a 2021 Stanford trial of 36 healthy adults, ten weeks on a diet rich in fermented foods such as yogurt and kefir raised gut microbiome diversity and lowered 19 inflammatory proteins in the blood, while a high fiber diet did not in that time. But it was small, it tested a whole diet, and kefir trials since then show modest, mixed changes.

In this video we look at what the Stanford researchers actually did, what live and active cultures means on a label, how kefir and yogurt compare, how much sugar can hide in a yogurt cup, what is still unknown for older adults, and five practical ways to use what the evidence supports.
