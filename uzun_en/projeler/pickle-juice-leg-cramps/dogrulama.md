## Summary
The script is accurate and well hedged. It says clearly that the study was in healthy young men with induced cramps and that no older-adult trial was found. It also frames the nerve-reflex explanation as a hypothesis. I made three small edits: the protocol timing in scene 16, the attribution in scene 42, and the removal of "awake" in scene 27. The sample size of the Miller study is not stated in the script, which avoids an unverified number. Approved.
