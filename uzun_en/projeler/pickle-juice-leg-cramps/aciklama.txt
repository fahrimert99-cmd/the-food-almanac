Does Pickle Juice Stop Night Leg Cramps?

In a 2010 lab study, a small amount of pickle juice ended electrically triggered foot cramps about 49 seconds sooner than water. But the volunteers were healthy young men, and we found no trial of pickle juice for night cramps in older adults. Here is what that study did and did not show.

We also look at why the effect may be a nerve reflex rather than an electrolyte refill, what a Cochrane review found about magnesium, the low-certainty evidence for stretching before bed, medicines linked to leg cramps, why quinine should be avoided, and five practical steps.
