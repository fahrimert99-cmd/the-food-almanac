MIND Diet and Memory After 60: What Studies Show

In observational studies at Rush University, older adults who followed the MIND diet most closely had a lower rate of Alzheimer's disease and slower memory decline. But when a three-year randomized trial in 604 adults aged 65 and older tested the diet against a control diet, both groups improved and the difference was not statistically significant.

In this video we look at what the MIND diet is, what the Rush cohort studies found, what the New England Journal of Medicine trial showed, why both groups may have improved, and which simple food habits, such as leafy greens and berries, have the best support. We also cover a safety note for people taking blood thinners.
