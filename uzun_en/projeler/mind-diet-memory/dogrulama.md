## Summary
Every figure and citation traced to its PubMed or PMC page. The script is balanced. It presents the observational findings as associations, and it states plainly that the randomized trial found no significant difference. Three small fixes were made:
- A caveat was added on the "11 years younger" figure.
- The bean and whole-grain frequencies were made exact.
- A qualifying strip was added to the thumbnail.

The hook and the promises (scene 3, "we'll come back" in scene 20) are paid off later. Not independently verified: the NEJM full text (403), so the weight-loss and practice-effect points rest on the investigators' comments in a news summary. They are framed as possible, untested explanations.
