Eat Vegetables First? What Food Order Really Does to Your Blood Sugar

Same lunch, same portions, same person. The only difference is the order you eat it in. In small clinical studies, eating vegetables and protein before carbohydrates lowered the rise in blood sugar after a meal. In this video we look at what those studies actually found, the three mechanisms that may explain it (gastric emptying, fiber and the gut hormone GLP-1), who benefits most, where the evidence runs out, and five practical ways to use it.
