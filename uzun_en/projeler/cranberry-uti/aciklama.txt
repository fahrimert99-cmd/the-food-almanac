Does Cranberry Juice Prevent UTIs?

Cranberry juice is one of the most popular home remedies for urinary tract infections, but does it work? A 2023 Cochrane review of 50 trials and more than 8,800 people found that cranberry products lowered the risk of symptomatic infections in women with recurrent UTIs, in children, and in people prone to infection after bladder procedures. It did little for older adults in care homes, pregnant women, or adults whose bladders do not empty fully.

In this video we look at what the review and two key trials found, juice versus capsules, the sugar in cranberry juice cocktail, the limits of the evidence, and the medicine caution for people who take warfarin. Cranberry is not a treatment for an infection you already have.
