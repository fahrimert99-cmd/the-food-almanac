## Summary
The script is accurate and carefully hedged. Every figure traced to the Cochrane summary, NCCIH, the JAMA release or the AJCN abstract. I made three edits: the GI side-effect wording now follows Cochrane's interpretation, the juice-aisle claim no longer says "most", and the JAMA citation is complete. The opening promises (care-home caveat, sugar catch, juice vs capsules, warfarin warning) are all paid off. The thumbnail number (−26%) appears in scene 1 and 18 and matches the review. Not independently re-opened: the retailer label and the commercial-involvement counts, both from the writer's notes.
