## Summary
Numbers, units and directions of effect all match the sources. The script was already well hedged on COSMOS (non-significant primary result, capsules not chocolate, hypothesis-generating). Four small wording fixes were made. Remaining caveat: the Miller data come from cocoa powders, not bars, and the script says so only indirectly (scene 37 says percentage is not a proxy). The title and thumbnail are honest. Approved.
