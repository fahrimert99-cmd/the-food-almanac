Is Dark Chocolate Good for Your Heart After 60?

In a trial of 21,442 adults aged 60 and older, a cocoa extract capsule was linked with a 27 percent lower risk of cardiovascular death. But the trial's main result, total cardiovascular events, did not reach statistical significance, and the capsules were not chocolate bars. This video goes through what that trial really found, what pooled blood pressure trials show, and why cocoa is not all the same.

We also look at how Dutch-processed cocoa loses most of its flavanols, what Consumer Reports found when it tested 28 dark chocolate bars for lead and cadmium, and five practical ways to read a US label and enjoy chocolate sensibly.
