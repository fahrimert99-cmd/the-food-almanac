Do Oats Lower Cholesterol? What Studies Show

Oatmeal has one of the oldest food-and-heart claims on record. In pooled analyses of randomized trials, eating at least three grams a day of oat beta-glucan, the soluble fiber in oats, lowered LDL cholesterol by roughly 7 to 10 milligrams per deciliter. That is a real but modest effect, and most trials lasted only a few weeks.

In this video we look at how beta-glucan works in the gut, what the FDA and European Food Safety Authority concluded, what two meta-analyses and one trial actually found, how many servings it takes to reach three grams, what we know and don't know about steel-cut, rolled, instant and overnight oats, and which toppings are worth a second look.
