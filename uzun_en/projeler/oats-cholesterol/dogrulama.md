Summary: the core premise (about 3 g/day oat beta-glucan lowers LDL modestly, ~7–10 mg/dL, short-term evidence) is well supported and honestly framed with limitations and safety notes. Hook promises (servings to 3 g, steel-cut/rolled/instant, toppings, overnight) are all paid off. Six minor fixes made.
