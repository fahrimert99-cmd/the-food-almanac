Walnuts and Cholesterol After 60: What Trials Show

In a two-year randomized trial of 628 adults aged 63 to 79, eating one to two ounces of walnuts a day lowered LDL cholesterol by about 4 milligrams per deciliter, with no weight gain. A pooled analysis of 26 controlled trials found a similar small drop. The brain results were less clear: the main memory score showed no difference.

In this video we look at what the Walnuts and Healthy Aging (WAHA) trial and the pooled analysis actually found, what walnuts contain, why swapping fats may matter, what the trial did and did not show for memory, what the FDA allows on labels, and how to measure a sensible portion in ounces and cups.
