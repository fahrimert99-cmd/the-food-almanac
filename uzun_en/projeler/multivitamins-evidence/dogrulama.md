## Summary
The script is accurate and cautious: it states the limits, includes the null results from PHS II cognition and cardiovascular outcomes, and gives safety notes. Six edits fixed overstatements:
- The PPI-only odds ratio is now labelled as PPI-only.
- The USPSTF stance on vitamin E and beta carotene is no longer limited to high doses.
- The vitamin D upper limit now has its unit and is no longer called "safe".
- The scene 3 promise was softened.
- The thumbnail number now says "~".
- A qualifier was added to the B12 safety statement.

Residual risk: some primary pages were blocked, so the PHS II and Baker figures rest on the notes and on figures I know from the published abstracts.
