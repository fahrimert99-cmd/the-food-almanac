Do Multivitamins Work After 60?

Do daily multivitamins do anything after 60? In the Physicians' Health Study II, a multivitamin did not prevent heart events and had at most a modest effect on cancer and cataracts in male doctors. In the COSMOS trials of adults over 60, it slightly improved memory scores over two to three years. Experts still call the evidence insufficient.

In this video we look at those trials, what the US Preventive Services Task Force concluded in 2022, why vitamin B12 and vitamin D may matter more than a combined pill, and five practical points, including medicine safety notes.
