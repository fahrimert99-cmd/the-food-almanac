## Summary
The sources are real and match the figures quoted, and the safety framing is good. The script states the limits of the evidence: observational, the 13 neuropathy cases, and the 13-year metformin result that was not significant. I made two small edits. Scene 15 now says the 15% AG share is among people not taking acid reducers. Scene 8 no longer rounds ">20%" down to "one in five". All forward references (the pill payoff, the blood-test advice, and "as promised") are paid off. Approved.
