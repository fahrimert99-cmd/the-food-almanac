Are 4-Day-Old Leftovers Safe After 60?

The usual advice says leftovers last three to four days in the refrigerator. But the clock starts when the food comes off the stove, not when it reaches the fridge, and some of the germs involved do not announce themselves. This video looks at what extension services, the American Heart Association, the FDA and the CDC say, plus a 1993 rice outbreak and a laboratory study on Listeria.

We cover the two-hour rule, the real fridge window, why a nose is not a safety test, how to cool a big pot, how to reheat, when freezing helps, and why food poisoning is riskier after 65. Five practical habits at the end.
