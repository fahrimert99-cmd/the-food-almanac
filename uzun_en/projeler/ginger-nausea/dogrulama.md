## Summary
The script is accurate and well hedged. Numbers, populations, study types and the thumbnail figure (−31%, which appears in scene 1 and 10) match the sources. Four small edits were made: dose-unit wording (scene 32), side-effect framing (39), a blood-sugar qualifier (47) and the added ginger-ale source. Hook promises (one symptom vs another, ginger ale, dose, who should ask first) are all paid off. Caveat: the Chaiyakunapruk and Lu statements are read from abstract summaries, and Ryan's 6-day schedule comes from the writer's notes.
