Does Ginger Really Help With Nausea?

A 2006 review of five small trials found that ginger lowered the risk of nausea and vomiting after surgery. A newer review with about four times as many patients found a more mixed picture: less nausea, but no clear drop in vomiting. So what does the research actually say, and does it matter whether you use tea, capsules or ginger ale?

In this video we look at the trials for nausea after surgery, during chemotherapy and in motion sickness, the doses that were actually tested, what a major ginger ale brand's label says about real ginger, and an important safety point for people who take blood thinners or have surgery coming.
