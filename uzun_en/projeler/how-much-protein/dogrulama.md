## Summary
Every citation was real and matched its claims on number, population and design. No fabricated or mismatched sources were found. The fixes were wording only, in these places:
- Scene 1: the outcome is lean mass, not muscle.
- Scenes 2, 30, 46, 57: the timing and 1.6 g/kg claims were stated more firmly than the evidence allows.
- Scenes 34 and 37: the "90 g" total and the "one day" measure were imprecise.
- Scene 43: a qualifier was added to the KDIGO claim.

Two caveats remain:
- The KDIGO figures were confirmed only through secondary summaries, and the guideline text was not opened.
- The 25–30 g per meal figure is in the PROT-AGE full text, not its abstract.

The kidney safety note, the doctor/dietitian advice and the "observational, not proof" framing are all present. Verdict: approved.
