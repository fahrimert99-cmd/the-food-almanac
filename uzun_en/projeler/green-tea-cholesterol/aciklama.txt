Does Green Tea Lower Cholesterol? What Studies Show

Green tea has a real but modest effect on cholesterol. In a pooled analysis of 31 randomized trials, LDL cholesterol fell by about 4.5 milligrams per deciliter. Most of those trials used concentrated green tea extract in capsules, and for brewed tea alone the drop was smaller and less certain.

In this video we look at what is in a cup of green tea, what two meta-analyses and a year-long trial in 1,075 postmenopausal women found, how the effect might work, why no trial has shown fewer heart attacks, the liver enzyme warnings attached to high-dose extract, how much caffeine a cup contains, and what to know if you take warfarin, nadolol or atorvastatin.
