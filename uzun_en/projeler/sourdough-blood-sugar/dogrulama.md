## Summary
All nine citations are real and match what they are used for. Eight edits: five softenings or clarifications of effect wording (early rise, highest-dose vinegar, "may" instead of "probably", white sourdough in Mofidi, age-evidence wording), one citation fix. Main residual weaknesses are already disclosed in the script: small trials, low-certainty pooled evidence, confounding of flour and fermentation, and unvalidated shopping heuristics.
