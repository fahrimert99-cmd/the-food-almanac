Sourdough Bread: What It Does to Blood Sugar

In a small crossover trial of 16 adults with impaired glucose tolerance, sourdough bread produced a smaller early rise in blood sugar and insulin than bread made with baker's yeast. But other trials found no average difference, and a pooled analysis of 18 studies rated the certainty of the evidence as low to very low. This video looks at what those studies actually found, why fermentation and whole-grain flour may matter, and where the evidence runs out.

We also cover how to read a US bread label, since the federal bread standard does not define the word sourdough, and five practical tips for shopping and eating. For education only; if you have diabetes or take medicine, talk to your doctor or pharmacist before changing how you eat.
