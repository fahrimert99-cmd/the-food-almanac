Raw Honey vs Sugar: What It Does to Blood Sugar

Is honey kinder to your blood sugar than table sugar? A 2022 analysis from the University of Toronto pooled 18 controlled trials and 1,105 people and found small average improvements: fasting glucose about 4 milligrams per deciliter lower. But most of the evidence was low certainty, the glucose effect faded after adjusting for publication bias, and the trials were mostly in younger, healthier people.

In this video we look at what honey is made of, how its glycemic index compares with sugar, what the Toronto analysis found for raw and single-flower honey, why those results are shakier than they look, how a tablespoon fits into American Heart Association added-sugar limits, and five practical ways to use honey.
