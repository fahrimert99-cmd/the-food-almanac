Hidden Sodium: Where Most Salt Comes From

In a study of 450 American adults, about 71 percent of the sodium they ate was added to food outside the home, by food makers and restaurants, and only about 5 percent came from the salt shaker. National CDC data point the same way: bread, pizza, sandwiches, deli meat and soup are among the biggest sources, and most of it comes from store-bought and restaurant food.

In this video we look at what those studies measured, how to read the sodium numbers and 'low sodium' claims on a Nutrition Facts label, and what two trials in older adults found when people ate less sodium: a 2023 crossover trial in adults aged 50 to 75, and the TONE trial in adults aged 60 to 80. We also cover the limits of the evidence and five practical ways to cut back. If you take blood pressure medicine or have kidney or heart disease, ask your doctor about your own sodium number.
