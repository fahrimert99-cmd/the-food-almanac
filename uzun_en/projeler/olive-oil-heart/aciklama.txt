Olive Oil After 60: What It Does to the Heart

In PREDIMED, a randomized trial of 7,447 Spanish adults aged 55 to 80, a Mediterranean diet rich in extra virgin olive oil was linked to a 31 percent lower risk of heart attack, stroke or heart death than a low-fat comparison group. The trial was withdrawn and republished in 2018, and large US studies add useful but observational evidence.

In this video we look at what PREDIMED and the Nurses' Health Study and Health Professionals Follow-up Study actually found, how much olive oil people used, what the FDA says, what lab tests show about cooking with extra virgin oil, where the evidence runs out, and four practical ways to use it.
