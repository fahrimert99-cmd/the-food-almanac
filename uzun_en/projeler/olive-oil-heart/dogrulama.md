## Summary
The core numbers match their sources. Six small edits:
- softened a causal verb in scene 1,
- corrected the FDA 70% oleic wording and its on-screen note,
- removed an unverifiable attribution in scene 6,
- softened an overreach in scene 44,
- removed one unused source.

The script already carries its main caveats: association not cause, the retraction, lab-only cooking data and the calories note. The hook is intact and every promise (amounts, cooking safety, the withdrawal) is paid off.
