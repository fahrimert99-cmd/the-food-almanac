## Summary
The script is well hedged: it separates association from cause, labels the mouse data as animal evidence, and includes the WHO caveats and a safety note for people with diabetes. Five fixes were made: the erythritol PMC ID, the ATVB and stevia citations, the DayTwo sentence, and the stevia sample size. No numbers in the narration needed correcting. Approved.
