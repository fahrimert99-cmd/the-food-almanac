Do Artificial Sweeteners Raise Blood Sugar?

Do diet drinks and sweetener packets raise your blood sugar? Pooled analyses of dozens of short randomized trials found no rise, yet a 2022 trial in 120 healthy adults found that two weeks of saccharin or sucralose slightly worsened blood sugar responses, while aspartame and stevia did not. The evidence is mixed, and it depends on the sweetener, the person and how long it is used.

In this video we look at those trials, a small stevia study in type 2 diabetes, the 2023 World Health Organization guideline and the American Diabetes Association advice, and the erythritol and blood clotting studies. We end with five practical points, including a safety note for people with diabetes or heart disease.
