Can Spinach Protect Against Macular Degeneration?

In two large US studies of about 100,000 adults over 50, people with the highest lutein and zeaxanthin intake had about 40 percent lower risk of advanced macular degeneration. That is an association, not proof, and the famous AREDS2 eye vitamins help only a specific group. This video walks through what the studies actually found.

We cover the macula, the Harvard cohort studies, the AREDS and AREDS2 trials from the National Eye Institute, why beta-carotene was dropped from the formula, who the pills are for and who they are not for, how much lutein spinach, kale and eggs provide, and what an eye-friendly plate looks like.
