## Summary
The script is well sourced. Every citation exists and matches its claims. The one factual error was in the egg-absorption study: it involved 10 men and lutein-enriched eggs, and the script said "10 volunteers" and plain eggs. I corrected that, softened one absorption claim, tightened the opening to say lutein and zeaxanthin, and made the thumbnail less promotional. Safety notes, caveats and forward references (the randomized trials in scene 17, the pills in scene 3) are all paid off later in the video.
