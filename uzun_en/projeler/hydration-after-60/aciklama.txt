Why Thirst Fades After 60: How Much Water Is Enough

After 24 hours without water, healthy men in their late sixties and early seventies felt less thirsty and drank less than young men, even though their blood was more concentrated. That small 1984 study, a 1976 study of kidney function across ages and a 2015 Cochrane review help explain why thirst can be a weak guide after 60.

In this video we look at what those studies found, what the US National Academies say about total water including food, coffee and tea, why a fluid limit for heart failure or kidney disease is a separate question for your care team, and how drinking earlier in the day may mean fewer trips to the bathroom at night.
