## Summary
All eight sources exist and the key numbers match. The main problem was the thumbnail ("–2%" fractures), which implied a benefit from a non-significant result and appeared nowhere in the video; it is now 0.98, as in the chart. Other fixes softened overstated framing (food "beats" pills, five-year treatment, "exactly the people", "high-dose", the single care-home trial) and added a small-subset qualifier. Safety notes (kidney stones, ask your doctor, never stop osteoporosis medicine) are present. The hook promises are paid off.
