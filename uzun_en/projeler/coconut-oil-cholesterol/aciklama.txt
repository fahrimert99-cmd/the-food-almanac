Does Coconut Oil Raise LDL Cholesterol?

A 2020 analysis in the journal Circulation pooled 16 clinical trials and found that coconut oil raised LDL cholesterol by about 10 milligrams per deciliter compared with non-tropical vegetable oils, and raised HDL by about 4. A smaller trial in healthy adults with an average age of 60 found coconut oil raised LDL less than butter, and not clearly differently from olive oil.

In this video we look at what is actually in coconut oil, what those two studies measured, why the claim that coconut oil is full of medium-chain fats does not carry over from MCT oil, what the American Heart Association concluded in 2017, where the evidence runs out, and five practical ways to cook with it, or without it.
