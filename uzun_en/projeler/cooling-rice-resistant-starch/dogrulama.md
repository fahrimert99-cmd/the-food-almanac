## Summary
The core claims match the sources. Numbers, populations and designs are accurate, and the script is hedged appropriately (small trials, healthy adults, borderline p value, parboiling twist, no data in diabetes). Fixes: the Lu storage description (overnight, not 24 h), the "healthy"/"prediabetes" framing for the potato trial, the specificity of the Pugh review wording, a softer parboiling statement, and a clearer thumbnail. Promises in scenes 2–3 and 19 are paid off. Residual caveat: the PubMed and PMC full texts could not be opened directly, so some numbers rest on the writer's notes.
