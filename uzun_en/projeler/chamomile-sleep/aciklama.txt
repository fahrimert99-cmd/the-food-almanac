Can Chamomile Tea Help You Sleep?

Older adults taking chamomile extract for four weeks rated their sleep better than those on a dummy pill. Yet adults with long-term insomnia, on a similar extract dose, showed no clear difference. This video walks through the small trials in older adults, new mothers and people with insomnia, and what two review papers found when they pooled them: some help for sleep quality, little evidence for insomnia itself.

We also look at how chamomile's compound apigenin might work (so far only in mice and lab tests), the possible role of a warm-drink routine, cautions for people with ragweed or daisy-family allergies, blood thinners such as warfarin, and sedatives, and four practical tips.
