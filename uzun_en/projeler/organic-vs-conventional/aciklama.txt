Is Organic Food Healthier? What Studies Show

A large French study linked heavy organic eating with about 25 percent lower cancer risk. A Stanford review of 237 studies found little nutrition difference. Both can be true, because they measured different things. In this video we look at what the research shows: nutrients in organic and conventional produce, pesticide residues compared with legal safety limits, the NutriNet-Santé cohort and why it cannot prove cause, the Dirty Dozen list, and how organic prices vary.

The short version: organic produce carries fewer detectable residues, but nearly all conventional produce tests at or below legal limits, and no long-term study has shown that organic food improves health. We finish with five practical ways to spend your grocery money where it may matter most. For education only, not medical advice.
