## Summary
The core claims are accurate and carefully hedged.

- **Corrected:** the NutriNet-Santé sensitivity analysis, the Hyland 95% figure, and two over-absolute phrasings.
- **Fine as written:** the cover figure (–30%) matches scene 18 and the Stanford source.
- **Sources:** none appear fabricated.
- **Not verified:** the 2% and "three-quarters below 0.01%" figures from Winter & Katz, the Baranski per-compound figures other than flavanones and anthocyanins, and the "3 clinical studies" figure. The PMC full texts were blocked, so these rest on the research notes.
- **Approved.**
