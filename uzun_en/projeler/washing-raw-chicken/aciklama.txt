Should You Wash Raw Chicken Before Cooking?

Many of us rinse raw chicken before cooking, hoping to wash the germs away. Kitchen studies point the other way: rinsing can splash bacteria beyond the sink, and in a North Carolina State University study of 300 home cooks, kitchen sinks became contaminated whether or not people washed their chicken, and about one in four salads picked up bacteria from the raw chicken.

In this video we look at what the splash experiments and the home kitchen studies actually found, why only heat, 165 degrees Fahrenheit, makes chicken safe, why food poisoning can hit harder after 60, where the evidence is uncertain, and five simple habits to use instead of the rinse.
