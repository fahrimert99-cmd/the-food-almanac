Does Turmeric Help Knee Arthritis Pain?

In a four-week trial of 367 adults with knee osteoarthritis, a turmeric extract eased pain about as well as ibuprofen. Other trials against placebo found smaller gains, and a 2022 review of 15 trials called the evidence promising but uneven. In this video we look at what those studies actually found, why curcumin is so hard for the body to absorb, and what the research can and cannot tell us about the joint itself.

We also cover the safety points that matter for many older adults: rare cases of liver injury, a report of a warfarin interaction, and cautions for people with gallbladder problems. Ask your doctor or pharmacist before starting any turmeric supplement. This video is for education only and is not medical advice.
