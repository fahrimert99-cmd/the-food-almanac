Hook and promises: the 367 figure appears in scenes 1 and 10. The absorption promise (scenes 2 and 7) is paid off in scenes 32–37. The safety checks and the blood thinner promise (scenes 3 and 23) are paid off in scenes 38–45. The title and thumbnail ("Turmeric for knees?") are honest.

## Summary
The script is accurate to its sources and well hedged. Six minor edits were made: a wider-uncertainty note on the 9 mm result, funding wording, scope of the MRI finding in two places, an NCCIH safety qualifier, and the attribution of the liver risk to bioavailable products. No sources were removed. Gaps in my checking: the PubMed pages and the EMA PDF could not be opened, and the hospitalisation and piperine counts rest on the writer's notes.
