## Summary
The core premise holds: frozen vegetables are often comparable to fresh vegetables stored for days, and sometimes better for vitamin C. The script already notes the limits: nutrients rather than health outcomes, beta-carotene, possible industry funding, and the 35-day broccoli caveat. Four wording or logic edits and three citation fixes were made. No fabricated sources were found. The thumbnail ("FROZEN VS FRESH?") makes no numeric claim.
