Are Frozen Vegetables Less Nutritious?

Frozen vegetables often get treated as the second-best choice. In lab comparisons, vitamin C in frozen vegetables was often similar to, and sometimes higher than, vegetables kept fresh in the fridge for days. In one broccoli experiment, 35 days of chilled storage cost about 39 percent of the vitamin C, and freezing also cost some, mostly during the quick heating step before freezing.

In this video we look at what happens to vegetables after harvest, what the head-to-head studies found, the one nutrient where some frozen vegetables fell behind, how boiling, steaming and microwaving change the picture, and five practical tips for buying and cooking both.
