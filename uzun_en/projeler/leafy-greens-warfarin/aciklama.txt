Warfarin and Leafy Greens: Avoid or Keep Steady?

If you take warfarin, you may have been told to stay away from spinach, kale and broccoli. But in a small randomized crossover trial of 12 patients, cutting vitamin K intake by 80 percent pushed the average INR clotting score up from 2.6 to 3.3, and adding vitamin K lowered it. The advice from official drug information is to keep your vitamin K intake consistent from week to week.

In this video we look at how warfarin and vitamin K work against each other, what Franco and colleagues, Khan and colleagues, and Sconce and colleagues found, how much vitamin K is in common greens, why newer blood thinners such as apixaban and rivaroxaban have no vitamin K diet rule, where the evidence is thin, and five practical ways to eat greens safely on warfarin.
