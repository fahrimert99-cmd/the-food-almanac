## Summary
Core premise (steady vitamin K intake, not avoidance) is well supported by MedlinePlus and the Franco and Khan studies, and the script is candid about limits. Five edits: the unverifiable Sconce "33 of 35" figure, an over-strong inference from Sconce, a source attribution for Swiss chard, and a timing detail for the Franco INR values. Hook promises and forward references (INR target in ARISTOTLE, newer drugs, five tips) are all paid off. Approved.
