## Summary
All numbers match the primary abstracts or FDA page. Four small edits: Dicken's population wording (two places), Chen follow-up span, and softening the drinks claim. Hall PMC and Srour PMC full text were blocked, so eating-rate, fat-mass, PYY and cohort age/sex details rely on the writer's notes. The Dietary Guidelines claim is supported only by secondary summaries and is stated cautiously. No fabricated citations found. Hook and forward references ("we'll come back to that", "which ones matter", five tips) are all paid off.
