Does Vitamin C Prevent or Shorten a Cold?

Vitamin C has a reputation as the cold fighter, but what do the trials show? A Cochrane review pooled 29 comparisons with 11,306 people. Taking vitamin C every day did not clearly cut how many colds the general public caught, and colds were only about 8 percent shorter in adults. Starting after symptoms began showed no consistent benefit. The exception was people under brief, heavy physical stress, such as marathon runners, skiers and soldiers.

In this video we look at what the review found, what happens if you start late, the one group that did benefit, what we know and don't know about orange juice, and why high-dose supplements deserve caution: in one Swedish study, men taking them were about twice as likely to develop kidney stones. We finish with four practical points.
