## Summary
The script matches the Cochrane summary I could open. One real error: RR 0.97 applies to the general-population trials (10,708 people, 24 comparisons), not the full 29/11,306 pool that includes the stress subgroup. I fixed scene 14 and added "general public" in scene 54 and the description. Kidney stone, severity and Peters claims are properly hedged. Promises ("8 gram number", "the twist") are paid off in scenes 24, 28 and 43. PubMed, NIH ODS and Mayo pages were inaccessible, so those claims rest on the writer's notes.
