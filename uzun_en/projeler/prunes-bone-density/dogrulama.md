## Summary
The script matches its sources closely; numbers, populations, effect directions and caveats (density not fractures, secondary/lab-based inflammation results, young constipation cohorts, 41% dropout) are accurate. I made six small edits: scene 22 (comparison with dried apple, not a within-group gain), its on-screen note, scene 39 (partial randomization), scene 58 ("slowed" instead of "cut"), and two incomplete citations. Safety notes (warfarin, potassium, diabetes, not a replacement for bone treatment) are present. The USDA figures were taken from the writer's notes and not re-opened.
