## Summary
The numbers match the sources. The main fixes were hedging the resveratrol figures and removing the "less is probably better" attribution, which the AHA fact sheet doesn't state. The Millwood and Semba details could not be re-opened because of the PubMed block. The AHA is still cited as a 2025 Circulation statement (first author possibly Piano), which I did not confirm.
