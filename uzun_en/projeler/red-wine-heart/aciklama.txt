Does Red Wine Protect Your Heart After 60?

For decades, a daily glass of red wine has been called a gift to the heart. In this video we look at where that idea came from, why the older studies behind it are now doubted, and what a 2023 review of 107 studies and about 4.8 million people found: no significant drop in the risk of dying for light drinkers, and higher risk at heavier levels.

We also cover the 'sick quitter' problem, genetic evidence on stroke, how little resveratrol a glass of wine actually contains, what the American Heart Association says about alcohol today, and five practical points for adults over 60.
