## Summary
The script is well hedged and matches its sources. It states the limits of the evidence (small healthy-volunteer studies, drug levels rather than outcomes) and includes the dissenting letter with its disclosed industry ties. I made five minimal fixes:
- "safe" fruits became "lower risk".
- The simvastatin design was clarified.
- "inactivate" became "can inactivate".
- The unsupported "Valencia" example was removed.
- A wrong source title was corrected.
