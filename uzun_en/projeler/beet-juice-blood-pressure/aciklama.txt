Beet Juice and Blood Pressure: What Studies Show

Across 22 trials pooled in a 2017 meta-analysis, people who drank beet juice had systolic blood pressure about 3.5 points lower than comparison groups. Results in patients with high blood pressure are mixed: one four-week trial found a drop of about 8 points, while a one-week trial in treated patients found none.

In this video we look at how dietary nitrate becomes nitric oxide with help from bacteria in your mouth, what the main trials found, how long one glass lasts, why antibacterial mouthwash may blunt the effect, the kidney stone caution, and five practical, safe ways to approach it.
