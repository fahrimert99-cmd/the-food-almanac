## Summary
Nearly all numbers match their sources, and the script is appropriately hedged: it flags the conflict of interest, the contrary null trial, and that mouthwash and oral-health claims are inferences. Fixes: thumbnail and scene 1 now say −3.55 as in the body, the diastolic figure is corrected, the unverifiable bottle-nitrate figures and attribution are removed, and two phrasings are softened. The unused NIDDK and Gee citations were dropped. Remaining caveat: Bahadoran details, including the 3.22 dose figure, rest on search abstracts and the writer's notes, since the full text was blocked.
