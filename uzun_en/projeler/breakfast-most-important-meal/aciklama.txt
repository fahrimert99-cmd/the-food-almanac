Does Skipping Breakfast Raise Blood Sugar in Type 2 Diabetes?

Is breakfast really the most important meal of the day? Randomized trials found that eating it did not help adults control their weight. But in a small crossover trial of 22 adults with type 2 diabetes, skipping breakfast made the blood sugar response after an identical lunch about 37 percent higher.

In this video we look at where the slogan came from, what trials say about weight and metabolism, the second-meal effect on blood sugar, what research says about protein and muscle after 60, why the evidence on heart disease is only an association, and how to think about the timing of morning medicines such as metformin and levothyroxine.
