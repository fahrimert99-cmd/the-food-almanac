## Summary
Sources are real and numbers match. The main issue was scope: the title and thumbnail implied that skipping breakfast raises blood sugar in everyone, but the evidence is one small trial in type 2 diabetes. That is now stated. Smaller fixes covered the Sievert detail, attribution, the Rong wording, the prediabetes advice and slogan certainty. Approved.
