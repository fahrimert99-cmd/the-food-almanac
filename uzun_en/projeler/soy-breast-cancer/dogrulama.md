# Fact-check: soy-breast-cancer

Abstracts opened via Europe PMC (Shu 2009, Nechuta 2012, Taku 2012, Dong & Qin 2011) and the ASCO Post page. The mean intakes (3 / 46 mg), the 8% aromatase-inhibitor figure and the Vanderbilt 11 g figure were not in the abstracts I opened; they rely on the writer's notes (full text and the VUMC release). The AICR page was not opened.

## Summary
All numbers, populations, directions of effect and study designs match the sources. Framing is appropriately hedged (association, not cause; supplements vs food; non-significant mortality stated). No edits needed.
