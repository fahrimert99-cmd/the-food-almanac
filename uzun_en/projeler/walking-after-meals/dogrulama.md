## Summary
The script is honest and well hedged. All headline numbers match their sources.

I read DiPietro, Dunstan, the Hashimoto summary and the Reynolds figures directly. PubMed was blocked by a cookie wall, so I took the Buffey and Reynolds numbers from secondary pages that agree with the research notes.

I made 5 small fixes:
- the DiPietro comparison baseline,
- the DiPietro walk timing,
- Dunstan's participant description,
- Dunstan's sitting wording,
- the Hashimoto DOI.

I found no fabricated sources. The script clearly says the Reynolds trial compares timing rather than walking against no walking. It is also candid about the small trials and the missing long-term data.
