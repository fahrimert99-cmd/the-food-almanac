What a 10-Minute Walk After Meals Really Does to Your Blood Sugar

A ten minute walk after dinner sounds too simple to matter. In small clinical trials, short walks after meals lowered the rise in blood sugar after eating, and in one randomized crossover trial of 41 adults with type 2 diabetes, three ten minute walks beat a single thirty minute walk taken at any time. The effect was largest after the evening meal.

In this video we look at what those trials actually found, how working muscles take up glucose without relying on insulin, how soon after eating to start, who benefits most, and what a walk cannot do. We finish with five practical, low effort ways to use it and a safety note for people on insulin or with heart conditions.
