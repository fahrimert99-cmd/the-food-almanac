## Summary
Numbers for all trials and meta-analyses matched the abstracts/summaries. Fixed four items: a causal-sounding thumbnail/scene-2 framing of observational fish data, an ambiguous thumbnail metric (–15% is CHD mortality), an unclear on-screen unit, and an inaccurate capsule description. Caveats: REDUCE-IT and AHA news figures were confirmed only through secondary summaries, but are consistent with the known published values. Approved.
