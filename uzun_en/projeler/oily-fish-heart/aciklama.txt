Sardines vs Fish Oil Pills: Heart Health After 60

In VITAL, a trial of 25,871 older Americans, a daily fish oil capsule did not significantly lower the combined risk of heart attack, stroke or heart death. Yet people who eat fish regularly are linked with lower heart risk, and one high-dose prescription form of omega-3 did reduce events in a specific group. Why the difference?

In this video we look at the large supplement trials (VITAL, ASCEND and STRENGTH), the prescription exception (REDUCE-IT), the link between eating fish and heart disease, what the American Heart Association recommends, how the FDA ranks sardines and salmon for mercury, and five practical ways to fit fish into your week.
