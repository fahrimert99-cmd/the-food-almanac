Could the sequence of your meal change what happens to your blood sugar?
The setup
One meal.
Two days.
Bread and juice first on one day. Chicken and vegetables first on the other. The carbohydrates came fifteen minutes later.
Same food · same portions · same person
A small 2015 study
The result
was striking.
−37%
blood glucose one hour after the meal
when vegetables and protein came first
What might explain it?
Three possible
mechanisms.
Fiber
Protein
Slower gastric emptying
These may change how quickly glucose reaches the bloodstream, and may affect gut hormones such as GLP-1.
Important context
Not a
magic trick.
The studies were small. Effects can differ from person to person. This is not a substitute for medical care.
Evidence is a reason to test a practical habit—not a promise of a cure.
Try the sequence
Vegetables
and protein
first.
Then eat the starch: bread, rice, pasta, potatoes, or something sweet.
